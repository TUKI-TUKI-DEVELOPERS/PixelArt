# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Peru who want to preserve a meaningful trip, celebration, family story, or personal milestone in a printed photobook. They often arrive without a ready-made design and need to understand, quickly and confidently, what PixelArt will create and what they need to provide.

## Product Purpose

PixelArt turns personal memories into printed books and photobooks. The custom-photobook route must make a bespoke cover feel understandable and attainable: the customer shares the story and references, PixelArt prepares the complete cover, and the customer later organizes the interior photos in the editor.

## Brand Personality

Personal, capable, and vivid. It should feel like a knowledgeable print studio that gives a personal story physical form—not a generic template marketplace or a cold production portal.

## Anti-references

Avoid empty copy-only hero sections, generic SaaS card grids, decorative AI gradients, and design that implies a customer receives a finished cover before PixelArt has reviewed the request. Do not obscure the complete-cover promise behind long explanatory text.

## Design Principles

1. Show the physical result before explaining the form.
2. Make the complete cover tangible: front cover, spine, and back cover are one deliverable.
3. Explain the customer's next step with direct, low-pressure language.
4. Preserve the emotional value of the story while making the production process feel credible.
5. Use visual hierarchy to reduce uncertainty before asking for information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, descriptive text for non-decorative visuals, and no essential motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
